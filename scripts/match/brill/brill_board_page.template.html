Contract, By, Result, Score Morty AI bids South; North, East and West make the calls the Brill robots made. Brill's robots then play all four hands. Score is North-South's.
Pct / IMP Our score against every real table of the board: matchpoint percentage in MP events, average cross-IMPs in IMP events.
DD The double-dummy result of our contract and its North-South score.
DD_EV The expected North-South score of our contract when the two hands of the non-declaring side are dealt at random (40 deals, each solved double dummy).
Par The double-dummy par score and contract.
Par_EV The DD_EV of the par contract: its expected North-South score by the same random deals. With several par contracts, the highest of their expected scores. The solver names a par contract at the lowest level that earns the par score (1♠ making nine tricks, not 3♠), and that contract is the one valued.
Result from "Brill table": the Brill entrant had our auction, or played our contract, in the event. "Autoplay": Brill's service played our auction now. "DD, autoplay pending": not played yet; the double-dummy result stands in.
